Max & Me

Everything your dog needs, on one simple map: dog parks, vets, pet stores, groomers, shelters, water bowls, bag dispensers, and trash cans.

Big text and big buttons. Neighbors add the small things with a photo taken on the spot, a reviewer checks each one, and anyone standing at a place can confirm it’s still there.

Coming soon to iPhone. Android and a version for this website will follow, sharing the same map.
