What’s on the map

Every kind of place has its own color and its own picture, so you never have to tell colors apart.

  • Dog parks
  • Pet stores
  • Vets
  • Shelters
  • Groomers
  • Bag dispensers
  • Trash cans
  • Water bowls
  • Vaccine clinics
  • Vaccine & adoption events
  • Dog-friendly cafés

Plus hand-picked natural food, treats, and toys, and an Emergency Vet button that finds the nearest open vet in one tap.

How neighbors add places

  1. Stand at the place and take a photo with the app’s camera. There’s no photo library, so every photo proves it’s real.
  2. The phone checks your location at the moment you take it, so a pin can’t be dropped from the couch.
  3. A reviewer confirms it before it shows on the map. Anyone standing nearby can later tap “Still here?” to keep it fresh.

Neighbors earn points, ranks, and badges for the small things: a bag dispenser, a water bowl, a vaccine day.

Our promise

  • Free, forever. The map, search, Emergency Vet, and adding places are never locked.
  • No ads and no tracking. No pop-ups, no banners, no ad networks.
  • Plain and readable. Large type that follows your phone’s text-size setting.

About Max & Me

Max & Me is a small independent project, made for people who love their dogs and want the walk, the vet, and the park to be easy to find. Questions or ideas? We read every email.
