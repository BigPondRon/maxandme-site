Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

The short version. We collect only what the app needs to work: your email to sign in, the places you add, and the photo and location of each one. We don’t show ads, we don’t track you, and we never sell your information. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

This policy explains what the Max & Me app and this website (“Max & Me”, “we”) collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

If you only browse the map without signing in, we don’t ask who you are and we don’t create an account for you.

When you sign in:

  • Your email address. Used only to send your sign-in codes and to contact you about your account. Other people never see it.
  • The name you choose (“Name your neighbors see”). It’s shown as “Added by …” on places you add.

When you add a place:

  • The kind of place, its name, and any note you write.
  • A photo taken with the app’s camera at that moment. The app can’t open your photo library.
  • Your phone’s location when the photo is taken, how accurate it was, and where you placed the pin (always within about 40 meters of the photo spot). We use this to check that the place is real.

When you add a photo of a place that has none (many places from the public map don’t have one):

  • A photo taken with the app’s camera at that moment.
  • Your phone’s location when the photo is taken, and how accurate it was. We use this to check that you took it at the place.

As you use the app: your points and badges, problem reports you send, and “still here” checks you make. All of these are tied to your account.

If you email us (for example with “Tell us what’s wrong” in the app): your email address, what you write, and the app version and kind of phone the app fills in for you. You see all of it before you press Send. We use it only to answer you and fix the problem. If you ask to join the beta, we use your email (and, for Android, your Google account email) only to invite you to test the app.

How your location is used

  • The app uses your location on your phone to center the map, sort places by distance, and find the nearest vets. It asks your permission first, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • To show the map, the app asks our server for places in the area you’re looking at, or near you for search and Emergency vet. We use those requests to answer them, not to keep a history of where you’ve been.
  • When you tap “Yes, it’s still here”, your location is sent once to check that you’re standing at the place. We save that you checked it and when, but not your location.
  • The only location we keep is the photo spot of places and photos you add.

What other people can see

  • When a reviewer approves a place you added, everyone using the app can see it: its photo, name, note, location, and your chosen name.
  • When a reviewer approves a photo you took of a place, everyone can see the photo on that place’s page. Your name and the photo spot aren’t shown with it.
  • Reviewers can see places and photos waiting for review, including the photo spot, GPS accuracy, and your chosen name, and the problem reports people send.
  • Your email, points history, reports, and “still here” checks are not shown to other people. Reviewers can see reports and checks to keep the map accurate.

Kept only on your phone

Saved places and recent searches stay on your phone and are never sent to us.

What we don’t do

  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t share it for advertising.
  • We don’t read your contacts, photo library, or other apps.

Services we use

We use a few companies to run Max & Me. They handle data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, and photo storage.
  • Our email delivery provider sends the sign-in code emails.
  • Apple provides the map tiles shown in the app and the App Store. Tapping “Directions” opens Apple Maps, and “Visit their website” opens your web browser. Those follow their own privacy policies.

Public places such as dog parks and vets come from OpenStreetMap. We download that public data. We don’t send your information to OpenStreetMap.

We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you delete your account. Places that are on the map stay up so neighbors can keep using them.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to the Me tab, tap Delete my account, and confirm. We then delete:

  • your sign-in, email, name, points, and badges
  • places you sent that aren’t on the map yet, and their photos
  • photos of places you sent that a reviewer hasn’t approved yet
  • your problem reports and “still here” checks

Places of yours that are already on the map stay, with their photos, but your name is removed from them. Approved photos you took of other places stay on those places. Deleted information may remain in routine backups for a short time before those expire. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your name on the Me tab, turn off location in your phone’s Settings, and delete your account at any time.
  • You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email us and we’ll answer within 30 days.
  • California residents: you have these same rights to know, correct, and delete your information. We don’t sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms, and we won’t treat you differently for using your rights.

Children

Max & Me isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we’ll delete the account.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database rules let each person change only their own information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If we make a big change, we’ll tell you in the app.
